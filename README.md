# Nihar Maddina

Machine learning engineer in Tampa, FL. I build ML systems that have to work on real data: audio
fingerprinting, source separation and similarity search at Cai Creative
(Techstars), LLM automation for small businesses as a forward-deployed AI engineer at Atlas
Professional Services, and before that the backend of an agentic AI platform as a co-founder at
MyCove. Computer science at the University of South Florida.

What I work on most: music information retrieval, embeddings and similarity search, tool-using
LLM agents, and the engineering around them (evaluation, cost control, tests).

## Selected projects

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [agent-eval](https://github.com/niharmaddina/agent-eval) | Offline-first harness for evaluating tool-calling LLM agents: a deterministic tool sandbox, a 29-task suite in three tiers with programmatic checkers, a fail-closed tool loop with schema validation and budgets, scripted and live providers, reproducible reports, 81 tests. |
| [rl-lab](https://github.com/niharmaddina/rl-lab) | Reproducible reinforcement-learning baselines: DQN and PPO on CartPole, Acrobot and LunarLander with seeds, pinned configs, held-out evaluation, learning curves and a results table that reports the non-converged case as plainly as the converged ones. |
| [HideAndSeekGym](https://github.com/niharmaddina/HideAndSeekGym) | A small 3D hide-and-seek Gymnasium environment with a dependency-free backend for training and an optional PyBullet backend for visualisation, plus PPO baselines with learning curves. |
| [HackUSF](https://github.com/niharmaddina/HackUSF) | Skin-lesion classifier built in 24 hours at HackUSF 2025: ResNet50, DenseNet121 and MobileNetV3 fine-tuned on seven lesion classes, combined by majority vote. |
| [quant-strategy-framework](https://github.com/niharmaddina/quant-strategy-framework) | Swing and mean-reversion strategy research in Python: backtesting with legacy and VectorBT engines, indicator sets, frozen universes to limit survivorship bias, ML signal-quality utilities, 147 tests. With Tushar Gona. |
| [Liquidity](https://github.com/niharmaddina/Liquidity) | Next-day price prediction with a liquid neural network and a neural ODE, served by FastAPI over a websocket to a React chart (2024 research prototype). |

Work at Cai Creative, Atlas and MyCove lives in private repositories.
